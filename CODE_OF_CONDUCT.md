# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and
not the person, and do not harass or discriminate. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits or contributors that violate this. To report a problem,
contact the maintainer, Syed Ali Jaseem, at the email listed on their GitHub
profile.
